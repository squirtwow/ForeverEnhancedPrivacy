# Changelog

## 1.0.0

### Added
- First release: a private mode for streams and screenshots.
- Names swapped or hidden on frames, nameplates, tooltips, chat, both friends windows, guild, mail, the group finder and pop-ups.
- Your own name and a made-up BattleTag, in a colour you pick.
- An option to keep Trade, General and Looking for Group readable.
- Gold, zone, coordinates, clock and the world map hidden.
- Turn it on with a key, for screenshots only, or always; a minimap button and an optional small eye show when it's on.
- Reset all settings, on the Overview.

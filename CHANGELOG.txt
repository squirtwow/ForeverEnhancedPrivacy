# Changelog

## 1.1.0

### Added
- Your guild becomes: a made-up guild name in your own tooltip, your guild's row and your /who line while private.
- Whisper inbox: hidden whispers wait in an inbox; `/privacy inbox` or shift-click the minimap button to read them off stream.
- Be right back screen: a key or `/privacy brb` covers the screen with your message and how long you've been away.
- While private, the chat box says "Tell Mage:" instead of the name of who you're whispering.

### Fixed
- Quest, item and spell links in chat keep their words when they match a hidden player's name.
- Escape in the Overview's boxes keeps what you typed.

## 1.0.0

### Added
- First release: a private mode for streams and screenshots.
- Names swapped or hidden on frames, nameplates, tooltips, chat, both friends windows, guild, mail, the group finder and pop-ups.
- Your own name and a made-up BattleTag, in a colour you pick.
- An option to keep Trade, General and Looking for Group readable.
- Gold, zone, coordinates, clock and the world map hidden.
- Turn it on with a key, for screenshots only, or always; a minimap button and an optional small eye show when it's on.
- Reset all settings, on the Overview.
